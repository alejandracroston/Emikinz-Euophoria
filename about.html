A Little Bit About Emily-Ann Likolehua Miskipeka

Aloha, I'm Emily-Ann Likolehua Misipeka

Aloha, I'm Emily Ann Likolehua Misipeka. I'm 32 years old and of Native Hawaiian and Filipino descent, from the island of Oahu, Hawaii.

Inspired by my culture, I've started making leis as long as I can remember. I started making leis with my kumu hula back when I was just a little girl.

I danced hula all my life, and being a part of such a wonderful ohana, we would make different styles often, which were passed down to me.

I love being able to share it with the world; it really becomes part of what aloha means and our legacy.

With love and aloha, Emily-Ann
