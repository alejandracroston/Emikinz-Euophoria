History Of Emily-Ann

Emily-Ann Likolehua Miskipeka

The history behind is very simple. Theirs symbolism and meaning behind the leis, for any occasion you can gift one, every lei and what you use has a different meaning and the cultural impact that it has on a polynesian community shows the importance of our culture. It’s a tradition passed down through generations and I want to continue to pass it down, in this generation their are a lot of people in my culture that doesn’t know how or doesn’t have the time to do so and yes it’s very hard work behind the scenes but their is so much love poured in that honestly I enjoy doing it. It started off as me learning very young from my kupunas (elders) to my hula halau Ohana it’s been apart of my life and will always be.
